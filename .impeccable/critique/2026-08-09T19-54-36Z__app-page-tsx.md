#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The design feels highly specific and authored for this exact product. The combination of massive `font-display` clamping, cinematic GSAP entrances, WebGL ripple effects, and the thematic `ink` and `ivory` palette directly reflect the tattoo and fine art medium. This doesn't look like a generic SaaS template; the bottom-aligned hero structure and edge-pulled images give it a distinct editorial feel.

**Deterministic scan**: No issues detected by the CLI (`detect.mjs`). Browser visualization was skipped.

#### Cognitive Load
**Low cognitive load (0 failures)**. The layout uses progressive disclosure perfectly—each section handles one concept (e.g., Credibility in `StatsGrid`). Choices are minimized to a single "Start a conversation" CTA in the hero. Working memory demands are virtually zero.

#### Emotional Journey
The journey starts with a dramatic, cinematic entrance (Hero), establishing immediate authority and mood. The `StatsGrid` acts as a strong reassurance valley, offering hard proof of credibility ("10+ Years", "500+ Tattoos") without breaking the artistic tone.

#### What's Working
1. **Exceptional Typographic Hierarchy**: The massive clamped text in the hero (`VISUAL ARTIST + TATTOOIST`) combined with delicate `font-mono` accents creates a premium, exhibition-like feel.
2. **Motion as Brand**: Using GSAP for a slow, 800ms reveal and tying the `StatsGrid` images to scroll scrubbing makes the site feel alive without overwhelming the content.
3. **Thematic Consistency**: The `bg-ink` and `text-ivory` classes are used religiously, ensuring the dark-mode-first approach stays true to the medium.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Hero Contrast Risk**: The `mix-blend-multiply` overlay on the hero image could result in low contrast for the "Start a conversation" and body text depending on the background image's exact crop. 
  - *Why it matters*: Users might struggle to read the primary value proposition or locate the CTA.
  - *Fix*: Ensure text contrast passes WCAG AA, potentially by adding a subtle scrim/gradient directly behind the text block.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable polish`
- **[P2] Viewport Horizontal Overflow**: The negative margins (`-mr-16`, `-ml-16`) on the images in `StatsGrid` are designed to pull them to the edge, but without strict `overflow-x-hidden` on the parent `<main>` or `<section>`, this can trigger horizontal scrolling on certain viewport widths.
  - *Why it matters*: Horizontal scrolling on vertical pages feels broken and frustrating on touch devices.
  - *Fix*: Verify and enforce `overflow-hidden` at the section or page wrapper level.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable layout`
- **[P2] Bottom-Heavy Hero Cues**: The hero pins both the primary CTA and the main title to the bottom edge. While striking, users on short viewports might not realize there's more content (like the Marquee and StatsGrid) below the fold.
  - *Why it matters*: Users might bounce before discovering the rich credibility sections.
  - *Fix*: Add a subtle, animated scroll indicator, or adjust bottom padding (`pb-12`) to allow the next section to peek through slightly.
  - *Suggested command*: `$impeccable clarify`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent)**:
- The contrast ratio of the ivory text over the image in the hero might fail WCAG standards without a solid backdrop or stronger gradient.
- The `InteractiveHoverButton` needs to ensure it has a visible focus ring for keyboard navigation, as custom hover buttons often lack these states by default.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile)**:
- The `<br />` tags in the `StatsGrid` (e.g., "Diploma,<br />JK Academy") might cause awkward orphaned words on narrow mobile screens.
- The negative margins on the `StatsGrid` images might behave unpredictably or cause layout thrashing on mobile browsers with dynamic safe areas.

#### Minor Observations
- The FOUC fallback (`.opacity-0 { opacity: 1 !important; }`) is a fantastic touch for no-JS environments.
- Using `order-` utility classes in `StatsGrid` is a smart way to flip the layout between mobile and desktop seamlessly.

#### Questions to Consider
- Does the hero need to be so dark (`rgba(0,0,0,1)` fading to `0.25`), or could the ripple effect carry the mood with a lighter touch?
- What if the `StatsGrid` felt more like a free-flowing exhibition wall rather than a rigid bento grid?
- Should the "Start a conversation" CTA appear again immediately after the credibility section to capture users once trust is established?
